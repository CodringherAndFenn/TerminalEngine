## 1. The kill order: the pairs (user, M33 round 1)

The four ocean zones make two pairs: **Paradise (heaven, north) against Pandemonium (hell, south)**, and **Pangaea (the past, west) against Panopticon (the future, east)**.

**In each pair, the one you kill last wins.** It outlived its rival, so it holds that balance. The final is **the two winners fused**, so there are **four finals**:

| Last of Paradise / Pandemonium | Last of Pangaea / Panopticon | The final |
|--------------------------------|------------------------------|-----------|
| Paradise | Pangaea | **heaven + the past** |
| Paradise | Panopticon | **heaven + the future** |
| Pandemonium | Pangaea | **hell + the past** |
| Pandemonium | Panopticon | **hell + the future** |

Example: the order N, W, S, E (Paradise, Pangaea, Pandemonium, Panopticon). Pandemonium fell after Paradise, so hell wins its pair; Panopticon fell after Pangaea, so the future wins its pair. The final is **hell + the future**.

**(mine)** The order is per run and shared by every player in co-op (like quest progress).

**The rule is hidden (user, round 8).** Nothing tells players in advance: no giver hints at it and the quest log doesn't name a pair's winner. Players work it out by playing. Each pair is settled by its first kill (its rival is then sure to fall last), so it can be learned. **(mine)** To make it learnable across runs, the victory screen and the journal record **the kill order that led to each final** (section 7).

## 2. Waking (user, M33 round 1)

**It wakes at once when the fourth ocean boss falls.** The sky changes over the whole map, an announcement names what is coming, and the final is pinned on the map at once. You can still take your time getting to it, though it comes for you anyway (section 3).

**(mine)** It **comes ashore on the main island's coast between its two realms**: heaven + the past from the north-west, heaven + the future from the north-east, hell + the past from the south-west, hell + the future from the south-east. The fourth boss falls out in an ocean zone, so its first leap (section 3) usually brings it to the players there.

## 3. Map-wide: it hunts you (user, M33 rounds 1-2)

**There is no arena and there are no walls.** The final roams the whole map toward the nearest player, so **wherever you are becomes the battlefield**. Its moves happen wherever it stands.

**It leaps to you (user, round 2).** It walks slowly toward the nearest player and **leaps when you get far ahead**: it crouches (the tell), takes off, and **lands near you with a big, telegraphed impact**.
- **(mine)** It walks at about half a hero's pace (~4 tiles/s; a hero runs 8.5). It leaps when the nearest player is more than ~60 tiles away: a 1 s crouch, then its landing spot is marked within ~12 tiles of that player 1.5 s before it lands, and the impact is a ring that you roll through or get out of.
- **(mine)** It leaps over water too, so it follows you out to the ocean zones. It never stops hunting while a player is alive.
- **(mine)** Its position is always pinned on the map and the minimap, with an edge arrow when it's off screen, and its bar shows on the HUD from the moment it wakes (the fixture rule, `BOSSES.md` boss direction: big effects and the boss must always be seen).

## 4. What it does to the island (user, M33 round 2)

**Its realms spread along its path.** The ground it walks turns into its two realms' land (for example blood-marble and chrome for heaven + the future). It smashes the trees and walls in its way, and **its realms' creatures crawl out of the trail**. **The scar stays for the rest of the run.**
- **(mine)** The trail is a band ~6 tiles wide, patches of each realm's ground mixed along it. Every leap leaves a crater of its land where it lands.
- **(mine)** The creatures are its two realms' own (from `OCEAN.md`'s rosters), endgame tough like on their islands, a few at a time every so often from the trail behind it, so a long chase leaves the land behind you dangerous.

## 5. The island's own monsters (user, M33 round 2)

**They stay, and they can fight it.** Everything carries on as usual. Its blows hurt them too (friendly fire), so **leading it through a biome's monsters turns them on it**, which you can use as a tactic.
- **(mine)** Like the existing friendly-fire rule (an enemy turns on another after taking enough damage from it), an island monster its attacks hurt turns on the final; its realms' creatures from the trail never do.

## 6. Its forms (user, M33 round 2)

**Two forms, each a full kit like the ocean bosses** (about 3 signatures stacked over the phases plus 4 moves, the Nameless Magus's shape scaled up):
- **Form 1: both realms side by side.** It shows both of its realms' natures, with signatures from each.
- **Form 2: fused.** At 0 the two realms fuse into one bigger thing, with its own full kit.

## 7. The ending (user, M33 round 1)

**Beating the final is the true victory.** It ends the run on a new victory screen that **names the final and shows your kill order**. The journal (the archivist's shelf, `GUILD.md` 4.4b) records which finals you've beaten. **Adventurer's Glory's early "End run (victory)" stays as the lesser victory** for players who stop before the finals.
- **(mine)** Dying during the final is a game over like any other death. The run records keep the kill order and the final either way.

## 9. The Fossil Angel's kit (user, M33 round 4)

### 9.1 Form 1: its bones walk

Its stone bones walk, wrapped in jungle and fire, under a halo of lava. **Signatures (all four), one added per phase** (**(mine)** four phases, 100-75-50-25-0%, like the hunger and the first life):
1. **Stone feathers** (heaven): it beats its stone wings and feathers rain down along marked lines. They **stick in the ground as stone spikes** for a while: cover for you, walls it smashes through.
2. **The lava halo** (heaven): its halo lifts off and rolls out as **a burning wheel circling it at a growing radius**, leaving fire behind. Slip through the gap, or hit the wheel until it **cools to stone** and falls.
3. **Echoes** (the past): Pangaea's twist (`OCEAN.md` 25): **its attacks replay 3 s later from where it stood**, at full damage. Because it keeps moving, each echo comes from a spot it has already left.
4. **Amber** (the past): it weeps resin that **runs out in rivers and hardens**. Caught, you're stuck until you roll free; hardened amber stays as walls.

**Moves (all four; it keeps walking through most of them):**
- **Wing sweep:** a stone wing rises (the tell), then sweeps a wide arc; roll under.
- **Stone dive:** a short hop, then a dive along a marked line, landing in a shower of stone (not its long hunting leap).
- **Ash breath:** a cone of ash and embers from its jaw; the embers keep burning on the ground a while.
- **Falling stars:** stones from the first sky fall on marked circles around you, in quick chains.

### 9.2 Form 2: the first angel unveils

At 0 its flesh grows back, red and raw. **Signatures (all four), one added per phase** (**(mine)** four phases):
1. **Eyes in the flesh** (heaven): eyes open across its new flesh and **each sweeps a gaze beam**. The eyes are weak points: **hit one to close it**.
2. **Life from its blood** (the past): its blood drips and crawls; left alone, **a drop grows** (like Pangaea's evolving life, `OCEAN.md` 28.2) **into a small unveiled angel** (a cherub, a dove). Kill the drops early.
3. **Red amber** (fused): its blood hardens like resin. **Pools hold you** until you roll free, and **walls of it rise across your path**.
4. **Six wings:** six new wings of raw flesh and feathers **beat in turn**, each beat a push wave and a volley of bloody feathers.

**Moves (all four):**
- **Claw rake:** three raking lines, shown first; stand between them or roll.
- **The first word:** it speaks, and **a ring of white light expands across the land**. Its stone is the only safe place: **hide behind feather spikes or amber walls**; caught in the open, it hurts a lot.
- **Blood rain:** it shakes its wings, and blood falls on marked circles that leave pools.
- **Halo wheels:** its halo splits into burning wheels that race out in straight lines and bounce off walls.

**(mine)** The first word needs cover to exist: form 2 keeps the stone feathers from form 1 falling (fewer, as the cover the word needs), and red amber walls count as cover too.

## 10. The Recording Angel's kit (user, M33 round 5)

### 10.1 Form 1: the seraph and its archive

A seraph wired into a server-throne, its six wings racks of screens, its halo a scanning ring. **Signatures (all four), one added per phase** (**(mine)** four phases):
1. **The book of deeds** (fused): it writes down what you do. **Anything you kill near it rises again as a witness of light** that fights you for a while, so leading it through monsters makes more witnesses. **(mine)** "Near" = within ~25 tiles of it; a witness lasts ~20 s, and one already risen never rises again.
2. **The scanning halo** (the future): its halo sweeps a wide cone like a camera. **Each second it sees you fills a JUDGED meter**; full, **a pillar of light falls on you**. Break its line of sight behind terrain to drain it. **(mine)** The meter shows on the HUD like the alert (`OCEAN.md` 42).
3. **Seraph fire** (heaven): its wings burn and **rings of fire spin out from it, each with a gap**, three at a time turning opposite ways.
4. **Redaction** (the future): it erases lines of the world. **Black bars sweep across the ground**, shown first by a blinking cursor; caught, you lose a chunk; walls and trees in a bar's path are erased for a while.

**Moves (all four; it keeps moving through most of them):**
- **Wing blades:** its six screen-wings fan out and swipe in three arcs, one after another.
- **Laser quill:** it writes with a beam; the cursor traces a line across the ground first, then it burns.
- **Trumpet blast:** a trumpet note in a cone that pushes you back and hurts.
- **Falling pages:** pages of light fall on marked spots around you and cut where they land.

### 10.2 Form 2: the judgement engine

Angel and archive fused. **Signatures (all four), one added per phase** (**(mine)** four phases):
1. **On trial** (fused): **it marks one hero for 6 s and every attack it makes goes at them**. Get through untouched and you're **acquitted: it staggers, open to hits for a few seconds**.
2. **Wheels within wheels** (heaven): chrome wheels with eyed rims roll out from it, race across the land and bounce, turning a little to follow you.
3. **Rapture beam** (heaven): **a column of light from the sky locks onto one hero and follows**; slower than you, but it speeds up the longer it lasts. Keep moving; roll when it closes.
4. **Deletion** (the future): **squares of the land flicker, then blink out into black void** for a moment; in one when it goes, you take big damage, and terrain and monsters inside are deleted.

**Moves (all four):**
- **The gavel:** it slams a hammer of light down: a ring spreads and a crack runs along a shown line.
- **Wing beams:** its six wings fire beams in a fan that sweeps across.
- **Page storm:** a spiral of razor pages whirls out from it.
- **Ascent:** it rises out of reach (its shadow stays on the ground), then crashes down on a marked spot nearby: a short-range cousin of its hunting leap.

## 11. The Golden Calf's kit (user, M33 round 6)

### 11.1 Form 1: the idol and its tribe

A giant living aurochs (the cave-painting bull) wearing the gold idol; an early tribe dances after it in a trance. **Signatures (all four), one added per phase** (**(mine)** four phases):
1. **The dancing tribe** (hell): the tribe dances after it in a trance, and **while they dance around it, it takes half damage**. They ignore you until you come close or hit one, then **the whole tribe frenzies** (Pandemonium's groups, `OCEAN.md` 14). Breaking the dance strips its shield, at a price. **(mine)** The tribe is ~8 dancers, made new from the trail every so often; "around it" = within ~10 tiles.
2. **The idol's shine** (hell): near the gold idol **Pandemonium's temptation meter fills**; full, you're entranced and drift toward the calf; a roll breaks it.
3. **The painted herd** (the past): **cave paintings come alive**: ochre bulls and hunters run across the land along marked lanes, with gaps to stand in.
4. **Tar wallow** (the past): it drags **tar** out behind it in sweeps; tar slows you, and **its tribe's fire-bearers set it alight**.

**Moves (all four; it keeps moving through most of them):**
- **Gore charge:** head down, it charges along a marked line, horns first.
- **Hoof stamp:** a shock ring spreads out, and cracks run along shown lines.
- **Horn toss:** close up, it hooks you on its horns and flings you; roll away when it lowers its head.
- **Spear volley:** its tribe throws a volley of spears at you, aimed as one and shown first as lines.

### 11.2 Form 2: molten gold and hunger

The gold melts into the beast. **Signatures (all four), one added per phase** (**(mine)** four phases):
1. **Molten trail** (fused): it bleeds molten gold; **its trail burns while liquid, then hardens into gold** that stays.
2. **Gilding** (hell): its glare gilds: **a GILDED meter fills while you stand in its gaze**; full, you're **a gold statue for a moment**. Get out of its sight to drain it.
3. **The golden herd** (the past): its dripping gold casts **small golden calves that charge you in packs**.
4. **Fool's gold** (hell): it scatters gold over the land. **Each piece is worth real loot to pick up, but fills your temptation**: a risk you choose.

**Moves (all four):**
- **Molten charge:** a charge along a marked line that leaves a line of molten gold behind.
- **Gold geyser:** gold erupts under you on marked spots, in chains.
- **Melt splash:** it shakes, and molten gold splashes out in a ring of droplets with gaps.
- **Devour:** it bites at a marked spot; anything there (monsters, its own tribe) is eaten and heals it.

**(mine)** Two meters at once (temptation from the shine and the fool's gold, gilded from the glare) show side by side on the HUD, like the alert and the judged meter.

## 12. The Feed's kit (user, M33 round 7)

### 12.1 Form 1: the screen tower and its crowd

A towering machine of screens and the feasting crowd it feeds. **Signatures (all four), one added per phase** (**(mine)** four phases):
1. **What you want** (hell): its screens show each hero what they want; **your temptation meter fills while you're in front of its screens**; full, you're entranced and drift toward it; a roll breaks it. **(mine)** "In front" = inside the ~120 degree arc its screens face, within ~30 tiles.
2. **The audience** (hell): a crowd follows it, staring at its screens, and **every watcher heals it a little**. Hit one and that one frenzies at you; thin the crowd to stop the healing.
3. **The cameras** (the future): its cameras raise **Panopticon's alert** (`OCEAN.md` 36): CALM, then SEARCH (its drones and turrets come), then LOCKDOWN (it locks onto you and attacks faster). Break line of sight to cool it.
4. **Pop-ups** (the future): **window boxes pop up on the ground around you**, each with a countdown and an [X]; shoot one to close it before it bursts; left alone, it blasts the area.

**Moves (all four; it keeps moving through most of them):**
- **Broadcast beam:** a wide beam of static from its main screen that sweeps across, shown first by the screen flickering.
- **Cable lash:** its feeding cables lash out along shown lines.
- **Delivery drops:** delivery drones drop parcels on marked circles around you; they burst.
- **Static ring:** a ring of static spreads out from it, with gaps to roll through.

### 12.2 Form 2: the walking mouth

Tower and crowd fuse into a walking mouth of screens. **Signatures (all four), one added per phase** (**(mine)** four phases):
1. **The scroll** (the future): **bands of the land scroll toward its mouth** like conveyors and carry you along; walk against them or roll across.
2. **The algorithm** (the future): it learns how you dodge: **its shots lead you**, aimed where your last few moves would take you. Change your pattern to beat it.
3. **Viral** (fused): **its static drives monsters into a frenzy that spreads** to the monsters near them, and the frenzied all hunt you.
4. **Hunger for more** (hell): it always wants more: **your temptation meter fills on its own**, faster the closer you are; entranced, you walk into its mouth and take a big bite.

**Moves (all four):**
- **Gulp:** its mouth lunges along a marked line and bites.
- **Spit-out:** it spits out what it ate: a fan of junk (screens, bones, scraps of food).
- **Cable tongue:** a tongue of cables lashes along a shown line, then grabs and pulls you toward its mouth; roll to break free.
- **Screen shatter:** it slams down, and its screens shatter out in a ring of glass shards.

**(mine)** "Viral" is form 2's answer to section 5 (the island's monsters fight the final): the Feed turns them on you instead, so with the Feed, leading it through monsters is a risk, not a tactic.

## 16. Built: M33.1 (2026-11-27) -- the kill order, the hunt, the Fossil Angel's first form

**The kill order** (`systems/finals.py`; `Quests.order` in `systems/quests.py`). Every ocean boss that falls goes on the order; the pair rule (`config.FINAL_PAIRS`, `FINAL_OF`) picks the final when the fourth falls -- six orders lead to each final. Nothing shows it (the rule is hidden): no log line, no hint. **Until a final is built, an order that picks it wakes the Fossil Angel instead** (`FINAL_STAND_IN`; the victory screen names what you fought, the journal keeps the real order). Each built final is a quest-like entry, `config.FINALS` (kind `"final"`: no giver, no camp), asleep and invisible until the order wakes it.

**Waking** (`Quests.wake_final`): the moment the fourth ocean boss falls -- the banner (**THE FOSSIL ANGEL**, "it comes ashore in the north-west -- and it hunts you"), the quest log's **FINAL** line, a map pin that follows it, its bar on the HUD and the edge arrow, and **the sky over the whole map** turns its colour (warm amber for the Fossil Angel: `FINAL_SKY`). It comes ashore **14 tiles inland from the main island's coast between its realms** (`FINAL_ASHORE`, `finals.ashore`) and stands there **10 s** (`FINAL_WAKE_GRACE`), then leaps to you.

**The hunt** (`ai/finals.Final`, every final's): its "lair" (`finals.Roaming`) is wherever it is, so the boss bar, the pin and the quest machinery work unchanged; the world is kept loaded round it (`FINAL_STREAM`, not while it comes ashore or flies). **(mine)** Because every hero is in its fight wherever they are, **the arena rules apply everywhere while it hunts: no mounts, no changing gear** (`systems/mount.in_arena`) -- like any boss fight, just map-wide.
- **The walk:** 4 tiles/s at the nearest hero (2 while a move is on), circling at ~7 tiles; it walks over water and through everything.
- **The leap:** when the nearest hero is more than 60 tiles off (90 mid-move): a 1 s crouch ("!" over it), then the landing ring shows 1.5 s ahead, 6-12 tiles from you, tightening and flashing; it can't be hit in the air; the impact is a 5-tile ring (40, pushing 4), then 0.8 s getting up.
- **The trail:** every tile it walks, the ground within 3 tiles turns to its realms' land (the Fossil Angel's: cloud, cloud drift and marble rubble; ash, basalt and fern meadow -- in patches of each), solid things smashed, water left alone; a 4-tile crater where it lands. The world remembers it for the rest of the run.
- **Its creatures:** **(mine, after the bench)** every 14 s, while fewer than 3 are about, 2 crawl out of its trail 8-30 tiles from it and from every hero (the Fossil Angel's: cherubs, doves, halos, lambs, raptors, sabre-tooths, dragonflies, spear hunters), endgame tough like on their islands.
- **Friendly fire:** an island monster its blows hurt turns on it at once (`ai/brain.py`); its own creatures it never hurts (`systems/combat._may_hurt`), and they never turn on it.

**The Fossil Angel, form 1** (`ai/fossil_angel.py`, drawn by `render/fossil_angel.py`, pictures in `render/finals_art.py`): 15,000 HP, four phases (100-75-50-25%), its body with a stone wing either side (folded, or beating up), a halo of lava ringing over its skull. The phases' banners: ITS HALO KINDLES, IT REMEMBERS, IT WEEPS AMBER.
- **Stone feathers:** 5 lines in a 70-degree fan, 16 tiles, shown 1 s; 22 a line; a stone spike every 4 tiles along each (`FEATHER_SPIKE`: cover, it breaks; never on a hero), standing 15 s.
- **The lava halo:** shown 1 s, then a burning wheel (`LavaHalo`, 500 HP at level 1, scaled like the angel) circles it, 3 to 18 tiles out over 10 s, a round every 3.2 s; it hits for 26 (pushing 2) and leaves fire every 2 tiles (4 s, 6 a tick). Hit to 0 it cools to stone (a boulder where it fell) and the halo is gone 8 s; otherwise it rolls back up at the end.
- **Echoes** (from the third phase): everything it does echoes 3 s later from where it stood (Pangaea's machinery, `ai/pangaea.Echoing`): the feathers' lines, the sweep, the breath, the dive (a ghost running its line), the shards, the stars. The wheel, the fire, the resin don't (they're the land, not attacks).
- **Amber:** it weeps 0.9 s, then resin runs out along 3 rivers (one at you), 14 tiles long, 2 wide, at 9 tiles/s; the running front holds whoever it catches 3 s (8 damage) unless they roll free; the resin hardens 2 s after it reaches a tile into amber walls (`AMBER_WALL`, not round a hero) standing 18 s.
- **Moves:** the wing sweep (a 150-degree cone 7.5 tiles out, 30, push 3.5, its tell following you 0.8 s); the stone dive (its line shown 0.8 s, a 0.4 s hop, 16 tiles at 22 tiles/s, 34, then a ring of 10 stone shards); ash breath (a 60-degree cone 12 tiles, 22, 4 embers burning 4 s); falling stars (2 chains of 4 stars round you, each marked 1.3 s, 1.8 tiles, 24).
